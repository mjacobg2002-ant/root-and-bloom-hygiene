Welcome

Dental hygiene that feels calm, personal, and yours.

Root & Bloom is an independent hygiene studio in Woodstock, founded by Katelynn Smith and Lauren Green, RDH. We deliver thorough, personalized preventive care — taking the time to listen, educate, and help you feel confident about your oral health.

  • Care planned directly around your individual needs
  • A calm, modern studio designed around your comfort
  • Independent hygienists — greater access to preventive care
What we do

Personalized hygiene care, every visit.

Preventive dental hygiene tailored to your individual needs — with in-office professional whitening coming soon.

Assessment

Hygiene Exams

New-client and recall exams that assess your oral health and shape care planned around your individual needs.

Preventive

Scaling (Cleaning)

Your professional cleaning — gentle removal of plaque and tartar, billed in 15-minute units of time.

Periodontal

Root Planing

Deeper care where bone loss has exposed the root — smoothing the surface to reduce bacteria and support healing.

Diagnostic

Digital Radiographs

Low-dose digital X-rays that detect changes in bone health early, for the best chance at long-lasting teeth.

Preventive

Fluoride & Desensitization

Topical fluoride and desensitizing treatments, used selectively to strengthen enamel and ease sensitivity.

Finishing & Education

Polishing & Coaching

Selective polishing for a smooth, healthy surface, plus personalized oral hygiene instruction for daily care at home.

Meet your hygienists

Founded by Katelynn & Lauren.

Two Registered Dental Hygienists with a combined 26 years in dentistry, Katelynn and Lauren built Root & Bloom to deliver preventive care their own way — personalized, unhurried, and centered on you. Both completed their dental hygiene diplomas with honours and hold local anesthetic certification from Dalhousie University.

KS
Katelynn Smith
Registered Dental Hygienist · Co-Founder

Katelynn has worked as a dental hygienist for 16 years. She studied at the University of New Brunswick and Oulton College, graduating with honours, and is certified in local anesthetic through Dalhousie University.

She's passionate about prevention and education — helping each client understand their oral health and feel genuinely confident in their care.

LG
Lauren Green
Registered Dental Hygienist · Co-Founder

Lauren has worked in dentistry since 2012 and as a hygienist since 2018. She completed her dental hygiene training in Ottawa with honours and holds local anesthetic certification from Dalhousie University.

She believes independent hygiene care means more time, more attention, and better access — care built entirely around the person in the chair.
